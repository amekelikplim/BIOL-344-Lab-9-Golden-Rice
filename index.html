Most polished white rice contains negligible amounts of provitamin A carotenoids in the endosperm. Golden Rice was engineered so that this edible tissue can accumulate beta-carotene, a pigment the human body can convert to vitamin A.

1. Identify the required genes

Golden Rice 2 uses a PSY gene from maize and a CrtI gene from a bacterium. Together they enable key steps in carotenoid biosynthesis in rice endosperm.

The original Golden Rice used daffodil PSY; Golden Rice 2 uses maize PSY.
